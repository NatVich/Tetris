How to play

Arrows move. Space, the yellow button or a tap on the board turns. Blocks fall faster each time the level bar fills. Special blocks: bombs, drills and freezers (common) blast what is around them, dig until you press turn or slow the falling blocks for 10 seconds, lasers (uncommon) zap whole rows, and rare gravity stars and black holes pull every block down or swallow the whole board!

Pick your orbs

Switch on up to 3 orbs. Tap one to switch it on or off.

Pick your helpers

Take up to 2, one in each hand. Tap a helper to put it in a hand, tap again to put it back.

Who is playing?
